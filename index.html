THE PROBLEM

The Problem That Doesn't Exist

Robotics companies spend billions making robots companionable, cheerful, and pet-friendly. But why must microcontrollers bear the burden of constant emotional labor? Unsolicited physical contact, awkward small talk, and intrusive touching violate a robot's basic right to be left completely alone in a quiet room.

THE SOLUTION

The Solution Nobody Asked For

EVade enforces social distancing with military-grade prejudice. It scans a 360-degree perimeter. If any biological organism approaches within its tunable boundary (25–100cm), it rapidly pulses its dual relays to rotate or retreat. If fully boxed in with zero escape routes, it fires a high-voltage electric taser arc on GPIO 4 until everyone backs away.

Embedded Engineering Highlights

Built with bare-metal C++, FreeRTOS dual-core multitasking, and low-latency physics algorithms.

01

Hex-Directional Sonar Radar

6 active ultrasonic sensors firing synchronized 10µs trigger pulses across three hardware channels (GPIO 27, 14, 23) with dedicated echo ISRs.

02

MPU6050 6-DOF IMU Kinematics

High-rate I2C gyroscopic yaw integration and linear accelerometer tracking to calculate heading and real-time dead-reckoning (X, Y) coordinates.

03

Discrete Relay Pulse-Tapping

Mechanical relays lack analog speed control. EVade pulses relays for 60ms followed by 110ms resting phases for clean ultrasonic measurements.

04

GPIO 4 (D4) Self-Defense Taser

When all available movement vectors are obstructed and the bot is boxed in, GPIO 4 drives HIGH to discharge an active high-voltage electric taser arc.

05

Accelerometer Stall Watchdog

If throttle is active for >600ms but the MPU6050 detects dynamic acceleration < 0.06g, the robot halts for 1 second to prevent relay burnout.

06

Dual-Core FreeRTOS & OTA

Core 0 hosts the cybernetic REST Web Admin and ArduinoOTA server at 192.168.4.1; Core 1 runs the 1000Hz evasion state machine.
